# Ideas

Things that might go into the app later. Not decided and not in priority order; each one needs looking into
before it is built.

- **Order from a builders' merchant.** Links to a store to order the cut list? Is there a web shop with an API
  that lets you put items in a shopping cart?
- **See a model in a realistic scene**, to get a feel for how it looks where it will be used: for example different
  rooms in a house, or an empty house. Perhaps upload your own photo, or take one with the phone camera directly?
- **Generated images.** Perhaps use ChatGPT (or similar) to create nice images with the model in them?
- **Share a link with a QR code**, so that the model is easy to open on another device.
- **Groups or components.** Group objects, or call them components. A component library: create components made of
  many objects that belong together. Perhaps a breadcrumb then, so you can see which level you are editing.
- **A critical look at the app.** Find the weaknesses a demanding user would find, then suggest improvements we can make.
- **Code quality behind the scenes.** The code should be of high quality; a critical reviewer should think it is good.
- **Transitions.** A fitting transition when a model opens, or when the "Material och färger" or "Om Bygg" modal
  opens. Nothing fancy or over the top: a touch of love and care that does not get annoying over time. Also when
  the detail panel is expanded and collapsed? (Unless it gets heavy performance-wise.)
- **Sign-out lands on Pocket ID.** After signing out you should come back to Bygg; now you seem to get stuck on
  the Pocket ID page.
- **Scenes.** Put several models in the same scene; the tool then behaves differently, working at a slightly higher
  level. One use: plan your flat by placing models and your furniture on an uploaded floor plan.
- **Import from elsewhere.** Read in a floor plan from Hemnet? Furniture models from IKEA, or free models on the web?
- **Edit lines and points** too, for fine adjustment?
- **Adapt the "realistic" render mode** to the hardware it runs on?
- **Assembly instructions**, so you get support when putting everything together later? Is it even possible?
